I am a <strong class="intro-title">Humboldt Research Fellow</strong> and postdoctoral researcher in the <a class="intro-focus" href="https://www.collaborative-ai.org/"><strong>Collaborative Artificial Intelligence (CAI) group</strong></a> at the University of Stuttgart, Germany, working with <a class="person" href="https://www.collaborative-ai.org/people/bulling/">Prof. Andreas Bulling</a>. Before joining Stuttgart, I was a visiting researcher and later completed a brief postdoctoral stint in <a class="person intro-understanding" href="https://users.aalto.fi/~oulasvir/">Prof. Antti Oulasvirta’s</a> <a class="intro-focus intro-understanding" href="https://cbl.aalto.fi/"><strong>Computational Behavior Lab</strong></a> at Aalto University, Finland. I received my Ph.D. from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). Before my Ph.D., I worked as a machine learning engineer at Baidu and as a deep learning researcher at Deepir, led by <a class="person" href="https://sites.google.com/view/stevenhoi/home">Prof. Steven Hoi</a>.

My research combines <span class="intro-field intro-field--hci">HCI</span>, <span class="intro-field intro-field--cogsci">cognitive science</span>, and <span class="intro-field intro-field--ai">AI</span>. I focus on <strong class="intro-focus intro-understanding">human understanding and cognitive modeling</strong>, and build on it toward <strong class="intro-focus">adaptive assistance and human–AI collaboration</strong>. My broader vision is to <strong>use AI to empower people toward true inclusion and equality</strong>.
