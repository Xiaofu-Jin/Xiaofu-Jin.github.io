{%- comment -%} Research structure diagram for the About section. Areas and their categories come from _data/research_topics.json. Under each category it lists the venues of the papers in _data/publications.json whose "category" matches (newest first), with award icons. Papers of the same venue share one chip ("ASSETS’23, ’22"), except that an awarded paper keeps a chip of its own so the award stays unambiguous. A paper with "hide_in_map": true is left out of the map (it stays in the publication list). Hovering a venue shows its paper titles. Set link_venues below to true to make each venue link to its (newest) paper in the publication list again. Categories with "ongoing": true get an "Ongoing" tag and a dashed branch. Each area's colors come from .research-area-- in _sass/hydeout/_research.scss. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign link_venues = false -%} {%- assign areas = site.data.research_topics | where_exp: 'topic', 'topic.categories' -%}
